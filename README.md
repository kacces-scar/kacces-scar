### About me

Hi, I'm **sunnysideup**. I study **General Engineering at King's College London**, and I'm especially interested in **industrial design**.

I'm drawn to the connection between engineering and design: how an idea becomes a physical object, and how its form relates to what it needs to do. I'd like to bring that perspective into the things I make.

This profile is a place to share my work as it develops. My public projects currently include an archive of CST antenna design iterations, keeping the steps behind a design alongside its final candidates.

### Exploring now

**Industrial design × engineering**

My focus is on industrial design and the engineering behind physical products. I'm interested in developing ideas through sketching, modelling, and iteration, with attention to both function and form.
